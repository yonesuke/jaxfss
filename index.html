jaxfss Reference Documentation

jaxfss is a neural finite-size scaling analysis package built on JAX, Flax, and Optax.

Finite-Size Scaling Analysis¶

The physical quantity near a critical point in a finite-size system obeys the scaling law:

A(T,L)=L−c2F[(T−Tc)Lc1]A(T, L) = L^{-c_2} F\left[ (T - T_{\mathrm{c}}) L^{c_1} \right]A(T,L)=L−c2​F[(T−Tc​)Lc1​]
(1)

where A(T,L)A(T, L)A(T,L) is a physical quantity at temperature TTT in a finite-size system of size LLL. TcT_{\mathrm{c}}Tc​ is the critical temperature, and c1c_1c1​ and c2c_2c2​ are critical exponents. Here F[⋅]F[\cdot]F[⋅] is the unknown universal scaling function.

jaxfss estimates critical points and exponents by parameterizing the scaling function with a neural network and optimizing it end-to-end.
